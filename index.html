FAQ

Questions

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Names, ticket numbers and progress are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Does this replace our HR process?

No. It covers the IT side: accounts, data, devices and access. Always check the steps against your company's own policies and retention rules.

What order should I do offboarding in?

Cut access first (sign-in, sessions, VPN), then handle data (mailbox, files), then hardware and licenses. The checklist is already ordered that way.

Can I add my own steps?

Custom steps and saved company templates are planned for Pro. Until then, copy the checklist into your ticket and add steps there.
